# Source model

**Internal Combustion Engine** by **T-FLEX CAD ST (Free)**, originally published on Sketchfab,
mirrored on Icosa Gallery by Devin Marble (07/02/2020): https://icosa.gallery/view/59QBP8GiT0q

Licence: **CC BY 3.0** (Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 International),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

`engine-gltf.zip` is the unmodified glTF download.

## Changes made in this repository

The build scripts in `../scripts/` turn it into a printable model: turned upright, scaled to 150 mm,
simplified and fused into a solid, hollowed, split into separate parts (sump, rocker cover, oil cap,
flywheel, pulleys, fan), and given new parts (motor sleeve, gears, blower, atomizer chamber, smoke channels,
exhaust stub). Generated files are derived from the original and are shared under the same CC BY 3.0 terms.
